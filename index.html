HELP CENTRE

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers for students and parents using the portal.

Ask another question
Is this an official government examination portal?

No. This is an educational information, learning and consultancy platform. It is not an official WAEC, NECO, JAMB, NERDC or Federal Ministry examination portal.

Can students practise CBT questions?

Yes. The frontend includes a sample interactive CBT engine. More school-approved questions can be added to its question bank.

Can I request admission assistance?

Yes. Complete the admission enquiry form and the request will be prepared for WhatsApp.

Can this website complete examination registration?

The frontend can collect an enquiry and connect you with the consultant. Actual registration should be completed through the relevant official examination body or approved channel.

Does the website store student information?

CBT history is stored locally in your browser. This frontend does not include a secure server or database for student accounts.
